# Stage 4 spectrum acceptance harness

The automated harness fixes the six required input cases: LEO, GEO and HEO,
each in `PEAK` and `CUMULATIVE` mode, with launch year 2026, five mission years,
SEP exceedance probability 0.1 and aluminium depths 0.01, 0.1, 1, 3 and
10 g/cm2.

The executable in-repository acceptance case is deliberately a contract case,
not a fabricated numerical gold baseline. It verifies product availability,
time and unit semantics, shielding metadata, explicit incomplete transport,
and source/transport grid convergence. Its ERB orbit sample count is reduced so
the normal test suite remains bounded.

Zero-shield identity remains a separate low-level invariant and is already
covered for SEP protons and HZE, GCR protons and HZE, and ERB protons and
electrons. Zero is not added to `ShieldingConfig`, whose public input contract
requires positive physical shield depths.

## Independent comparison and unresolved evidence

COSRAD is an independent implementation for cross-checking, not an
unquestioned numerical or physical truth standard (explicit user clarification,
2026-10-30). Agreement alone cannot authorize coefficient changes, remove
`INCOMPLETE`, establish data provenance or close a normative-release gate.
Disagreement alone does not prove a RADAR defect either. Match the physical
quantity, units, scenario, solar history, component and model assumptions first;
then resolve discrepancies against primary sources and independent checks.
Separate formula/data verification, numerical convergence and cross-code
comparison in every acceptance report. Any retained COSRAD arrays are labelled
comparison fixtures, not correctness oracles.

- COSRAD numerical results for the six cases have not yet been captured with a
  documented COSRAD version, settings and export precision.
- The helium NEK value `b_c` remains unresolved. A dedicated COSRAD comparison
  is one planned source of evidence, not sufficient authority to change it;
  primary-source review and a recorded resolution are also required.
- Missing or unverified HZE transport and LET rows remain explicit release
  blockers; the harness must not replace them with interpolation or invented
  expected values.
- ERB orbital-sampling convergence requires a dedicated long-running validation
  run. The OST energy nodes themselves are normative table nodes and are not
  synthetically doubled.

No LEO/GEO/HEO arrays or tolerances may be frozen as correctness gold values
merely because they were generated by COSRAD. Before recording a discrepancy,
declare the comparison scope, export precision and any unresolved differences
in physical models and solar/time conventions.

The separate long-running runner and its deterministic JSON schema are
documented in `docs/validation/stage4_reference_runner.md`. The runner uses the
same canonical scenario/configuration definitions as this bounded acceptance
harness, so the test matrix and manual validation command cannot silently
diverge.

The source-domain decisions and the independent endpoint/density/transport
checks are recorded in `docs/validation/sep_energy_domain.md` and
`docs/validation/gcr_energy_domain.md`.

The 2026-10-02 visual and independent mathematical audit of OST Appendix E is
recorded in `docs/validation/ost_appendix_e_formula_audit.md`. It documents the
E.3 full-duration denominator fix, the E.6/E.12 printed inconsistencies and
the explicit correction policy. It also keeps the current RAAN-ensemble versus
time-trajectory assumption as a separate release blocker; numerical density
agreement cannot close that methodological question by itself.

The same-day Appendix V audit is recorded in
`docs/validation/ost_appendix_v_formula_audit.md`. It maps V.1-V.14 to the
source implementation and explicitly keeps the AKL normalization, energy/time
conventions and nonpositive-gamma suppression unresolved. Those reasons attach
only to AKL contributors and their totals; the numerical spectra and the
pending NEK helium coefficient are unchanged.

## Verification record, 2026-10-02

The initial complete run took 3898.78 s: 1322 tests passed and six failed.
The failures were stale component-status/version/protocol-label expectations
and a mocked SEP loader test which did not isolate its cached records. The
expectations and cache setup/cleanup were corrected without weakening the
physical completeness or convergence requirements.

The repeat run passed 1336 tests in 746.67 s, excluding seven expensive
GCR/SEP domain and density checks. All seven passed in the complete run;
their numerical source and transport operators were not changed afterwards.
The late all-month GCR status/reference merge changed no numerical values and
was checked separately: 125 source/product/model/pipeline/loader tests and
35 Stage-4 release/peak-chain tests passed. The latest tree collects 1352
tests. This is combined regression evidence, not a claim of a single final
1352-test green run. Ruff, mypy (132 source files) and diff whitespace checks
also passed.

Both 40-comparison representative SEP/GCR protected-density matrices satisfy
the strict <1% integral criterion. This does not close isolated secondary
proton quadrature, the quarantined LET data, ion/table coverage, ERB trajectory
and sampling assumptions, AKL conventions, or the pending COSRAD comparisons.

The subsequent L/B-coordinate audit is recorded in
`ost_appendix_d_magnetic_coordinates_audit.md`. OST source version v3 adds the
missing L>5.5 shifted-dipole branch, corrects polar IGRF synthesis and requires
an actually located magnetic minimum. Region preclassification remains an

The same continuation added opt-in secondary-proton incident-energy quadrature.

depth/output-crossing integration error. The false-zero convergence claim was
removed, a bounded uncertainty path was added, and both default and opt-in

TENDL example is not a replacement for full GCR convergence. See the final
sections of `stage4_data_provenance.md` for evidence and regression results.

The subsequent bounded depth/coupled-incident partition correction is recorded
in `secondary_quadrature_crossings.md`. It resolves the analytic missed-window

The 2026-10-31 HZE continuation adds a separate diagnostic conservative primary
transport/electronic-LET adapter on the full mass-parameter candidate. All 84

The following independent He/Al,Si physical-data check is documented in the
ASTAR section of `stage4_data_provenance.md`. It compares 101 source-table
energies per material with exact native CATIMA evaluation as well as the
100/200-node candidate interpolation. Electronic-stopping interpolation differs
from native evaluation by less than 0.05%, but native-vs-ASTAR differences reach
-6.317593% in Al and +8.632568% in Si at low energy. This separates numerical
resolution from inter-model agreement; the existing 1% numerical criterion is
not a physical acceptance tolerance. Absolute range origins, all other ions,
energy-domain qualification and the complete orbital matrix remain open.
Neither source is treated as an error-free oracle, and no production defaults,
tables or release restrictions are changed.

The subsequent He spectral-impact check evaluates 144 fixed-source cases on
the common 0.01..250-MeV/n support, separating Al-range and Si-electronic-LET
changes at identical nodes. It preserves unsupported source contributions:
88.375% of the selected GCR He integral is above this support, versus less than
0.007% for the selected SEP cases. Small changes of a total integral do not
imply small changes of high-LET tails. The 101-node sparse-table witness also
does not establish a uniform 1% interpolation bound. Independent forward
quadrature and localized narrow-tail checks reproduce selected supported
integrals; all details, denominators and hashes are in the final helium-impact
section of `stage4_data_provenance.md`. No production table is replaced and
the six-case orbital acceptance matrix remains open.

The subsequent [experimental heavy-ion comparison](iaea_heavy_ion_stopping.md)
checks 2612 selected IAEA Al/Si records and seven measured RADEF Si checkpoints
without refitting or changing production tables. Exact-energy native controls
are separate from table interpolation, range and spectrum validation. Large
low-energy residuals and source correction notices remain unresolved, and
selected observations stop at 985 MeV/u in Al and 90.63 MeV/u in Si. This adds
physical-data evidence but does not qualify the complete HZE candidate or
remove any Stage-4 acceptance restriction.
